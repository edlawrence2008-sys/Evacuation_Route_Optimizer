================================================================================
  EmacEArt Stylized Grass - what is in this pack
================================================================================

OPEN IT
  project.godot            open this with Godot 4.7 or newer
  icon.svg                 the pack icon shown in the Godot project manager
  README.txt               how to run the demo, controls, every folder explained
  LICENSE.txt              the licence

SHADER
  Shaders/EA_GrassWind.gdshader
      One shader for the whole pack. Wind, gusts, the cut-out blade silhouette,
      two layers of colour variation and the winter dial live in it.

MATERIALS - four ready fields of grass
  Materials/EA_Grass_Green.tres     green summer meadow
  Materials/EA_Grass_Dry.tres       dry, straw coloured grass
  Materials/EA_Grass_Dead.tres      dead, brown grass
  Materials/EA_Grass_Winter.tres    winter grass with snow on the blades

MESH
  Meshes/EA_Grass_Clump.mesh
      One clump of blades. Every field in the demo is this clump repeated.

TEXTURES
  Textures/GRASS_DIFF.tga           colour and the blade shape
  Textures/GRASS_NMAP.tga           surface detail

SCENES
  Scenes/GrassDemo.tscn             the demo: three fields side by side, the
                                    winter field behind them and a panel of
                                    sliders. This is what opens first.
  Scenes/GrassSpectrum.tscn         five wind settings standing next to each
                                    other, plus a field going from calm to gale
  Scenes/UI_ParameterPanel.tscn     the slider panel used by the demo

SCRIPTS - demo only, none of them is needed to use the grass
  Scripts/GrassField.gd             fills a rectangle with clumps
  Scripts/DemoCamera.gd             the camera you steer with the mouse
  Scripts/WindSpectrum.gd           builds the five labelled groups
  Scripts/SpectrumCamera.gd         the fixed camera move of that scene
  Scripts/UI_ParameterPanel.gd      connects the sliders to the grass
